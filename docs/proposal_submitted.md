## 原文

We propose to investigate natural language guided selection of fashion instances: given an image and a Chinese or English description such as "the bag on the left" or "the red skirt," our system will return the target instance mask and bounding box. This supports interactive product exploration and requires distinguishing objects of the same category. Our three-person team will adapt public implementations and pretrained models while independently developing the data pipeline, grounding module, and course experiments. Background reading will cover Mask2Former, DeepFashion2, Fashionpedia, DINOv2, BGE-M3, and referring-expression comprehension methods such as MAttNet. Our core system will use a manageable subset of Fashionpedia mapped to eight categories: top, pants, skirt, outerwear, dress, shoes, bag, and accessory, excluding garment-part annotations. Single-source and balanced mixed-source comparisons with DeepFashion2 will be restricted to the five shared clothing categories under matched training budgets. We will preserve official split boundaries, group related product or duplicate images where possible, and reserve an untouched test partition before generating descriptions. We will construct Chinese and English descriptions using category, image-relative position, and manually verified visual attributes, review each for correctness and a unique target, and include manually authored test descriptions beyond training templates. We will fine-tune a pretrained Mask2Former-R50 model and first implement a category-and-position rule baseline. Our proposed extension is a lightweight region-text ranking network with learned projections of frozen DINOv2 ViT-S/14 region features and BGE-M3 text features, supplemented by normalized spatial features. Training will use target-description pairs and competing instances, emphasizing same-category negatives. We will ablate spatial features to examine their contribution to ambiguous-instance selection. Segmentation will be evaluated using COCO mask AP and per-category AP on fixed test images. Grounding will be evaluated using target-selection accuracy with ground-truth candidates as a diagnostic, and mean target-mask IoU and success at IoU 0.5 with predicted candidates, counting absent outputs as zero IoU. Results will be reported by language and query type, including scenes with multiple same-category instances. We will present comparison plots, qualitative overlays, failure cases, and inference latency for initial image processing and cached-image queries. We expect to characterize accuracy-cost tradeoffs without assuming performance improvement.
